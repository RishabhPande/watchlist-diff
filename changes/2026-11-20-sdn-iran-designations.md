## Matching notes

- Arabic-script forms printed on all four entries: native-script matching is needed to resolve even the primary names, not only aliases.
- Alias transliteration vowel shift: SERVATI / SARVATI is the same surname romanized two ways; a matcher keyed to one spelling misses the other.
- SERVATI's entry pairs a Dominica passport with an Iranian one: a second-citizenship document as an identifier on an Iran-linked designation, so document-country assumptions fail here.
- TAJ, Majid is a short common name: high collision potential for name-only matching.
